2. HOW THE ADMINISTRATOR CREATES NEW USERS & TECHNICIANS
================================================================================

1. Sign in with the Admin account (admin@mobilerepair.com / admin123).
2. Click "Users" in the left navigation sidebar (URL: /users).
3. Click the blue "+ Add New User" button in the top-right corner.
4. Enter the employee's details:
   - Full Name (e.g. "John Doe")
   - Email (e.g. "john@mobilerepair.com")
   - Password (use the Eye Icon to preview/check password)
   - Role: Select ADMIN, TECHNICIAN, or USER (Service Desk)
5. Click "Create User". The account is immediately created and ready to log in.

* Note: Admins can also reset passwords (Key icon) or deactivate accounts anytime.

3. COMPLETE SYSTEM USAGE WORKFLOW (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

STEP 1: INWARDING A DAMAGED PHONE (Front Desk / User)
----------------------------------------------------
1. Log in and navigate to "New Repair" (or click "+ New Repair" button).
2. Enter the device details:
   - Serial Number / IMEI (e.g. "SN123456")
   - Mobile Model (e.g. "iPhone 15 Pro")
   - Problem Description (e.g. "Screen flickering after drop")
   - Date, Additional reference ID, and any special notes
   - Assign to a technician or leave unassigned
3. Click "Submit Repair Ticket".
4. The system automatically creates a sequential ID (e.g., REP-2026-00005).
5. Click "Copy ID" on the confirmation popup to paste it on customer receipts.

STEP 2: CHECKING REPAIR HISTORY (Search)
----------------------------------------
1. Click "Search Repair" in the sidebar.
2. Enter the phone's Serial Number or Repair ID.
3. If the phone has been repaired before in the past, all historical repair
   tickets are displayed in chronological order (newest first).

STEP 3: WORKSHOP REPAIRS & STATUS UPDATES (Technician)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Technician logs in and opens the repair ticket from "All Repairs".
2. Click "Update Status".
3. Change status from "Pending" -> "In Process" -> "Completed".
4. Add technician diagnostic notes (e.g. "OLED screen replaced, tested OK").
5. Click "Apply Status Change". The popup updates and closes automatically.
6. The audit trail records:
   - Exact status change
   - Timestamp
   - "By: [Technician Name] (TECHNICIAN)"
   - Diagnostic notes

STEP 4: REPORTS & ANALYTICS (Admin)
-----------------------------------
1. Go to "Reports" in the sidebar.
2. Filter by date range, status, or specific technician.
3. Click "Export CSV" or "Export Excel (.xlsx)" to download reports.
